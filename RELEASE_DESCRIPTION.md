## Modrinth Description

# Enrichment Solution

**Enrichment Solution** is a focused, polished industrial and geothermal tech mod for **Minecraft 1.21.1 (NeoForge)**. Built around *"the basics done well"*, it features ore enrichment, circuit manufacturing, a closed-loop geothermal binary-cycle power plant, heat-aware conduits, and an autonomous mining robot — complete with an **in-game Guide Book**, **JEI support**, and **dedicated server compatibility**.

---

## Getting Started

Every player receives the **Enrichment Guide** automatically on their first world join (and extra copies can be crafted). Open it anytime for a complete walkthrough of ores, machines, recipes, the geothermal fluid loop, and the robot.

---

## Features

### Worldgen & Materials
- **Ores:** Natural world generation for **Steel**, **Bauxite**, and **Aluminium** ores (stone & deepslate variants), plus **Crude Oil** lakes and underground pockets.
- **Components:** Raw ores, ingots, Uranium processing, and **Machine Casings** as the shared crafting base.

### Processing Machines
- **Enrichment Chamber & Quad Enrichment Chamber:** Enrich Raw Uranium into Enriched Uranium. Place four Enrichment Chambers in a **2×2** to automatically merge into a **Quad Enrichment Chamber** multiblock with 4 parallel channels and auto-sort.
- **PCB Printer:** Manufactures **Robot, Basic, Advanced, Ultimate, and Ultra Circuits** using 3 unordered input slots.
- **Stamping Press:** Crafts **Pressed Aluminium** and **Mechanical Eyes**.

### Geothermal Binary-Cycle Power Chain
A closed-loop power system: `Crude Oil → Liquid Isobutane → Gaseous Isobutane → FE → Liquid Isobutane`.
1. **Fluid Pump** — Scans up to 64 blocks straight down to pump Water or Crude Oil.
2. **Oil Refinery** — Refines Crude Oil into Liquid Isobutane.
3. **Water Tank (Multiblock)** — Up to 20×20×20 with dedicated I/O ports and a heat multiplier calculated from depth, nearby lava/magma, and desert biomes.
4. **Heat Exchanger (Multiblock)** — Up to 20×20×20; uses hot water to boil Liquid Isobutane into Gaseous Isobutane.
5. **Geothermal Generator** — Burns Gaseous Isobutane at **20 FE/mB** (up to 1,000 FE/t) and outputs exhaust gas.
6. **Condenser** — Cools exhaust gas with cooling water back into Liquid Isobutane (1:1, no loss).

### Power & Storage
- **Solar Panel:** 60 FE/t clear / 15 FE/t rain. Right-click with a **Solar Upgrade Template** to upgrade it to the 2-panel advanced form (150 FE/t clear / 40 FE/t rain).
- **Energy Cells:** 4 tiers (Basic 1M, Advanced 4M, Ultimate 16M, Ultra 64M FE) with per-face I/O controls and retained energy when broken.

### Conduits & Tools
| Conduit Type | Tiers | Highlights |
|---|---|---|
| **Energy Cables** | Basic, Advanced, Ultimate, Ultra | 2,000 – 128,000 FE/t throughput |
| **Fluid Pipes** | Basic, Advanced, Ultimate, Ultra | 96.0% – 98.5% heat retention per block |
| **Thermal Pipes** | Isolated, Reinforced, Vacuum, Cryogenic | 99.5% – 100% heat retention per block |

- **Configurator:** Cycle `push / pull / none` on conduits and `input / output / disabled` on machine faces.
- **Hi-Tech Goggles:** Head-slot HUD displaying fluid name, amount, temperature, thermal multiplier, or cable FE storage within 4 blocks.

### Autonomous Robot
- **Robot & Robot Charger:** Powered by Energy Cell items; follows or stays, mines stone columns with an equipped pickaxe, collects drops, defends its owner, and uses a jetpack to close distance.
- **Auto Recharger Upgrade Template:** Lets the Robot automatically return to a **Robot Charger** when its cells run low.
